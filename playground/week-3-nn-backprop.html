Vanishing Gradients: Notice how the sigmoid and tanh derivatives approach 0 for large |x|. This causes gradients to shrink exponentially in deep networks, making early layers learn very slowly. ReLU mitigates this for positive values but can suffer from "dead neurons" when x < 0.
